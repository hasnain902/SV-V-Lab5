# Task 1 - Identify Operations

## Smart Museum Artifact Conservation System

| OP-ID | Operation | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| OP-01 | Perform Sensor Self-Check | To check that all essential sensors are working correctly |
| OP-02 | Record Artifact Information | To record the artifact identification information |
| OP-03 | Load Environmental Limits | To load the required environmental limits for the artifact |
| OP-04 | Monitor Environment | To continuously monitor temperature and humidity |
| OP-05 | Correct Temperature | To restore temperature to the permitted range |
| OP-06 | Verify Temperature | To verify that temperature has returned to the permitted range |
| OP-07 | Correct Humidity | To restore humidity to the permitted range |
| OP-08 | Verify Humidity | To verify that humidity has returned to the permitted range |
| OP-09 | Reduce Light Exposure | To reduce light exposure and protect the artifact |
| OP-10 | Generate Operator Alert | To inform the museum operator about a serious problem |
| OP-11 | Detect Vibration | To detect significant vibration inside the chamber |
| OP-12 | Suspend Risky Activities | To temporarily stop activities that may increase risk to the artifact |
